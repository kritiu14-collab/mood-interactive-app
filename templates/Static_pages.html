What is Solace?

Solace is a mood-interactive mental wellness web application designed to help students and young adults track, understand, and improve their emotional wellbeing. Built as a BCA Final Year Project in 2026, it combines mood tracking, AI companionship, community storytelling, and science-backed exercises in one compassionate platform.

💡 Solace is not a medical product. It is a wellness companion designed to support emotional awareness and healthy habits.

Our Mission

We believe mental wellness should be accessible, stigma-free, and human-centred. Solace exists to give every student a safe, private space to check in with themselves — to track how they feel, talk to an AI companion named Eva, and read stories from others who have found their way back from difficult places.

Features

  • Mood Tracker — Log your mood daily across 9 emotional states
  • Eva AI — A compassionate AI companion using Gemini, available 24/7
  • Healing Exercises — Science-backed CBT and mindfulness techniques
  • Community — Anonymous positive story sharing with animal identities
  • Streak and Badges — Gamified consistency rewards to keep you motivated
  • 30-Day Results — Personalised insights unlocked after consistent use
  • Mood Music — Curated Bollywood and international playlists by mood
  • Journal — Private morning, evening, and night reflection entries

Built With

Python (Flask) · SQLite · HTML / CSS

Team

Developed by a BCA student as a final year academic project. Designed with empathy, built with code, and driven by the belief that technology can be kind.

Eva is powered by Google Gemini and generates responses based on general wellness principles. Eva is not a licensed therapist, counsellor, or mental health professional. {% elif page == 'privacy' %}

Data We Collect

  • Account information: username and email address
  • Mood logs: the moods you select and when
  • Journal entries: what you write privately
  • Chat history: conversations with Eva AI
  • Task data: tasks you create and complete
  • Login dates: to calculate streaks and award badges
  • Community posts: stories you choose to share
  • Feedback: ratings and messages you submit

How We Use Your Data

  • To personalise your mood dashboard and experience
  • To calculate your login streak and award badges
  • To generate your 30-day results and suggestions
  • To power Eva AI with your current mood context
🔒 Your data is stored locally in a SQLite database. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except that your message and current mood are sent to Google Gemini API to generate Eva's responses.

Community Anonymity

When you post in the community, your real username is never shown. Only your assigned animal identity is visible to other users. Admin can see your real username for moderation purposes only.

Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage. We do not store plain-text passwords.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us or asking an admin.
