Face Fear Gently
Evidence-based exercises to reduce fear and build courage
Step 1🔍
Name It to Tame It
Say out loud: "I am feeling fear." Naming an emotion activates the prefrontal cortex and literally reduces the amygdala's alarm response in your brain.
Step 2📊
Fear Fact-Check
Write your fear. Then: What's the realistic worst case? Best case? Most likely outcome? Fear reliably shrinks when examined with actual facts.
Step 3🐢
Slow Exhale Technique
Inhale normally, then exhale TWICE as long. Long exhales directly activate the vagus nerve and send a safety signal to your brain.
1:00
Step 4🦁
Courage Anchor
Think of one moment you were brave — even something small. Hold that memory for 30 seconds. Your brain can't feel fear and pride simultaneously.
0:30
Step 5🚶
Move Through It
Walk around your space for 2 minutes. Fear is stored as physical tension. Movement tells your body the threat is not real and helps release it.
2:00
Step 6✍️
Best Case Journaling
Write in detail what your life looks like if the feared thing goes WELL. Spend 5 minutes here. Your brain responds to what you vividly imagine.
